# Pricing

**Owned by Ian.** Claude never quotes, discounts, or commits pricing. It fills a proposal's price fields with bracketed placeholders and routes to Ian.

## Current sheet

`[[FILL: tiers, price points, contract length, usage limits, overage terms. From Ian.]]`

## What Claude may say to a prospect about price

- Frame value, not numbers: what the data replaces (a scraping team, stale feeds) and what it unlocks.
- If pushed for a number, the move is: capture the ask in the `/customers` file, and route to Ian for the quote. Do not improvise a figure.

## What triggers a routing to Ian

Any request for a price, a discount, a custom volume, an SLA, or contract terms.
