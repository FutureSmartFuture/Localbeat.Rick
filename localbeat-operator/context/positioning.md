# Positioning

How we frame LocalBeat against the alternatives a prospect is weighing. Draft. Sharpen with real win/loss notes.

## The core line

LocalBeat is the national local-events corpus you would otherwise spend years and a team trying to assemble: 85M+ events, 300K+ venues, 180+ categories, US-wide, structured and forward-looking, delivered by API.

## The alternatives a prospect considers, and our answer

**Build it themselves (scrapers + manual curation).**
Their reality: hundreds of thousands of venue sources, constant breakage, no taxonomy, no dedupe, stale within days. Our answer: we already run that at national scale and keep it fresh. License it and put your engineers on your product, not on plumbing.

**General web search / LLM answers.**
Their reality: unstructured, unreliable, no coverage guarantee, not licensable as a feed. Our answer: structured records with venues, coordinates, timing, and taxonomy you can query and depend on.

**Single-category or single-city event APIs.**
Their reality: narrow, patchy, and they still have to stitch coverage together. Our answer: one national corpus, one integration, 180+ categories.

## Proof assets we lead with

- The canonical stats: 85M+ events, 300K+ venues, 180+ categories, US-wide.
- A live pull for the prospect's own market (the wedge).
- Honest coverage numbers (description fill, tagging, recurrence). We win on trust.

## What we do not claim

We do not claim 100% coverage, perfect tagging, or ticket links on everything. We state the real fill rates. Overclaiming is how deals die on the technical call.
