# Proof

The evidence we use to make value concrete. Ordered from strongest to supporting.

## Strongest: the prospect's own market

A live, cleaned, branded pull for their city and category. Nothing beats "here is your market, this week." Build it with the `market-pull` skill. This is the wedge in every stalled deal.

## Canonical corpus stats (US-wide)

- **85M+ events**
- **300K+ venues**
- **180+ categories**

Use these exact figures in marketing and sales. Do not round differently or invent new ones.

## Honest coverage facts (build trust)

- ~75% of events have descriptions.
- ~7% have ticket URLs (absence is a reasonable proxy for free/unticketed).
- ~20% arrive untagged.
- 65 to 90% of events in tested markets belong to a recurring series (3+ days/month).

Stating these upfront is a closing tool. It signals we know our data and will not surprise them on the technical call.
