# Objection library

The recurring reasons API-licensing deals stall, with a tested response and the proof for each. A routine mines `/customers` weekly and adds new ones. Keep responses short, honest, and proof-backed.

## "It's too expensive / we can't justify the cost."

Reframe to what it replaces. A national feed versus a scraping team plus manual curators plus stale listings. Attach a live market pull so the value is concrete, and offer the business-case one-pager the champion can forward. Route the actual number to Ian.

## "How fresh is the data? / Is it stale?"

Be specific and honest. The corpus is forward-looking and updated continuously. Show a live pull with recent and upcoming dates for their market. Do not overclaim a refresh SLA (that is Ian's to commit).

## "Do you even cover our market / our category?"

Prove it, do not argue it. Run a facet count for their city and category for the true total, then a cleaned sample. If coverage is thin there, say so and show where it is strong. Honesty here wins more than it loses.

## "Integration looks like a lot of work."

Lower the perceived effort. One REST API, structured records, one integration for national coverage versus maintaining scrapers for 300K+ venues. Offer a scoped starter pull and point their engineer at the `localbeat-api` basics.

## "We're not sure how we'd use it." / fuzzy value

This is a use-case gap, not a price gap. Pull the matching vertical use case from `context/icp-and-verticals.md`, tailor it to them, and attach a market pull that demonstrates that exact use.

## "We need to loop in [procurement / legal / a VP]." / champion can't sell up

The blocker is internal, so arm the champion. Business-case one-pager, forwarded-ready, in their language. Offer to join a short call with the budget owner. Keep the ask to one thing: time with the decision-maker.

## "We're comparing you to [alternative]."

Pull the answer from `context/positioning.md` for that alternative. Lead with the national-corpus-versus-DIY framing and the honest coverage facts.

## "Now's not a good time / circle back next quarter."

Do not vanish. Ask for the specific trigger ("what changes next quarter that makes this a yes?"), log it, and set a dated re-open. Meanwhile send one piece of value (a fresh market pull) so we stay concrete, not naggy.
