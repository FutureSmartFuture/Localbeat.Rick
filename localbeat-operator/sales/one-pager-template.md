# One-pager templates

Two one-pagers. Both follow `localbeat-brand`: white foundation, indigo identity, red for the single action, category colors for tags, Bricolage headline, Inter body, no em-dashes. Build the visual version with the `docx`/`pptx`/`canvas-design` skills as needed; this is the content spec.

## A. Vertical value one-pager (outbound)

- **Headline:** the outcome for that vertical ("Fresh local events, without the manual team").
- **The problem (2 to 3 lines):** their pain, in their language.
- **What LocalBeat is (2 to 3 lines):** national corpus, API, the canonical stats.
- **How they'd use it (3 bullets):** the vertical use case, concrete.
- **Proof:** a small market pull for a relevant city + the honest coverage facts.
- **One action (red):** book a 15-minute walkthrough.

## B. Internal business-case one-pager (for the champion to forward)

Built so a champion can send it to a budget owner unedited.

- **Headline:** the decision, framed as obvious ("Replace manual event curation with a national feed").
- **What it replaces:** current cost/effort in their terms (contractors, scrapers, stale listings). `[[FILL from their /customers notes]]`.
- **What they get:** coverage stats + their market pull.
- **What it costs:** `[[bracketed placeholder, Ian supplies the number]]`.
- **The ask:** approve a [trial / pilot / contract]. One line.
- **Risk addressed:** the honest coverage facts, so the budget owner is not surprised later.

Keep both to a single page. Lead with the prospect's own market whenever possible.
