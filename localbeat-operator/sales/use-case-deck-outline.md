# Use-case deck outline

Built from the branded six-slide use-case template (in the Brand kit under `Company/Brand/Deck Templates/`). Duplicate it per lead. The `use-case-deck` skill assembles this. Fonts embed (Bricolage headline, Inter body); cover and CTA carry the indigo wavy pattern fading from the right.

## The six slides

1. **Cover.** Outcome headline, client/vertical, date.
2. **Use case.** Challenge to outcome, two columns. Written in their language from `/customers`.
3. **How it shows up.** Four surfaces/touchpoints where the data appears in their product.
4. **Proof.** Dataset stats (85M+ events, 300K+ venues, 180+ categories, US-wide) plus a quote/proof block.
5. **Events in [Region].** The framed placeholder gets their real market pull (map-pins image or clean table). This is the slide that closes.
6. **CTA.** One next step + contact (Ian for terms).

## Rules

- Every slide has speaker notes; `[ ]` brackets mark placeholders.
- Do not overfill. White space is the brand. One idea per slide.
- Slide 5 must use a real pull for their market, not a stock example.
- QA note: Bricolage and Inter are not in the LibreOffice-safe font list, so leave ~10% headline slack and do not trust an automated text-fit check on headlines.
