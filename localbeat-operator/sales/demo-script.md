# Demo script

A 15 to 20 minute demo built around the prospect's own market, not a generic tour. The data does the selling.

## Before the call

Run a market pull for their top city and categories (see the recipe). Read their `/customers` file for the use case and the blocker. Have the pull open and branded.

## Arc

1. **Their world, in one line (1 min).** "You're trying to [their use case in their words]." Confirm.
2. **Their market, live (5 min).** Show the pull. "Here's what LocalBeat sees in [City] right now: [headline stat]." Click into a few records: venue, timing, taxonomy, recurrence. This is the moment that lands.
3. **Coverage, honestly (3 min).** The canonical stats (85M+ events, 300K+ venues, 180+ categories), then the honest fill rates. "We'd rather you know exactly what you're getting."
4. **How it plugs in (3 min).** One REST API, structured records, national coverage from one integration. Address their specific integration worry.
5. **The blocker, head-on (3 min).** Name it and answer it from the objection library.
6. **One next step (1 min).** A single clear ask: the technical call, the trial key, or time with the budget owner. Not "let me know your thoughts."

## After the call

Update the `/customers` file: what landed, the new blocker if it moved, the agreed next step and date. Draft the follow-up with the pull attached.
