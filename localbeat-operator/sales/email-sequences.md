# Email sequences

On-brand templates. Voice rules: title-case LocalBeat, short declarative sentences, no em-dashes, one ask per email, momentum at the end. Personalize every one from the prospect's `/customers` file. Draft only. Never auto-send.

Brackets `[ ]` are fill-ins. Keep emails short.

## Re-open a stalled deal (the flagship)

> Subject: [City] events, pulled fresh for [Company]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Rather than check in, I pulled what LocalBeat sees in [City] right now: [N] events across [categories] in the last [window], [one concrete detail: e.g. "including [X] recurring live-music nights"].
>
> This is the coverage your calendar would run on, updated continuously.
>
> Worth 15 minutes to walk through what this looks like for [Company]?
>
> [Signature]

Attach the cleaned market pull. One ask: the 15 minutes.
