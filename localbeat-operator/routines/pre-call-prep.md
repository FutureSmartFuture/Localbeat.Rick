# Routine: Pre-call prep

**Schedule:** evening before any booked prospect call (trigger per meeting, or a nightly check of tomorrow's calendar).
**Mode:** draft/prepare only.

## Prompt

> A prospect call is booked for tomorrow with [prospect]. Read their `/customers` file, `context/icp-and-verticals.md`, and `context/positioning.md`.
>
> Produce `precall-[prospect]-[date].md` with:
> 1. **One-line context:** who they are, the use case, the current blocker.
> 2. **A fresh market pull** for their top city and categories (use the `market-pull` skill / recipe). Cleaned, branded, with the headline stat. This is what you walk in with.
> 3. **Likely objections** for this prospect and the response for each, from the objection library.
> 4. **The one ask** to close the call on.
> 5. **Open questions** to ask them.
>
> Draft only. Route pricing to Ian.

## Output

A one-page brief plus a live, branded pull for their market, ready before the call.
